About DART Grid Atlas

An interactive map of U.S. power generation and transmission infrastructure.

Features

  • Power Plants Map — Operating, Planned, Retired, and Canceled/Postponed entries (including Puerto Rico), filterable by technology and capacity.
  • Infrastructure Map — 94,000+ transmission lines and substations, filterable by voltage.
  • ISO regions — regional boundaries shown for context.
  • Click any plant, line or substation for details. Light and dark themes.
